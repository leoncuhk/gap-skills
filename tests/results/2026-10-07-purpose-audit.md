# Public-purpose audit of recent changes

Scope frozen before this review's edits: `c7a2fab..084e9e0`, all 12 commits since the pre-collaboration baseline, plus the current public README, installed skill, package metadata and GitHub About text. Review the changes by their distinct purpose, not by whether a previous reviewer approved them. Existing historical reports remain evidence with their original scope; they are not rewritten.

Decision criteria:

1. Preserve the complete project purpose: help humans and AI complete real work, keep purpose/value/responsibility judgments with people, let the agent carry investigation/build/test/resumption/closure, and reuse experience only with evidence of scope and results.
2. Preserve the original method coverage and one progressively disclosed entry point. A clearer introduction must not silently remove a distinct purpose or capability.
3. Retain a mechanism only for an identified failure or reader operation. Distinguish a necessary correction, a useful optional mechanism and an unproven benefit. Ordinary small tasks must remain low-friction.
4. Public readers must be able to understand, obtain and use the package, and tell a current demonstration from historical or locally retained evidence. Claims must match actual code and checks.
5. Make the smallest justified corrections. No new framework, global install, browser workaround or comparative model experiment; preserve failures and do not relax acceptance criteria.

Main thread reviews goal/coverage changes and public metadata. Two fresh read-only reviewers independently assess public product fit and code/evidence integrity. Budget: one review and one final-diff recheck per reviewer, with safe deterministic probes only for concrete concerns. This audit cannot establish long-term user-effort reduction.
