# Notice

Gap-skills adapts ideas and mechanisms from the following MIT-licensed projects and public writing, largely via its direct predecessor [defog](https://github.com/leoncuhk/defog) by the same author. None of these authors were involved in, or have endorsed, this project.

- Matt Pocock — https://github.com/mattpocock/skills (MIT). Adapted: the `grilling` interview loop (one question at a time, facts-vs-decisions split, recommended answers, no action before shared understanding) and the `writing-great-skills` principles this repo is written by. The documented failure modes of its wayfinder (#450, #484) informed gap-skills's observable-trigger and launch-packet rules.
- Thariq Shihipar — "A Field Guide to Claude Fable 5: Finding Your Unknowns" (claude.com blog). Adapted: the map/territory framing and the four-quadrant unknowns taxonomy.
- Neeeophytee — https://github.com/Neeeophytee/finding-unknowns-skills (MIT). Adapted: belief-labeled prototype variations, the reaction-to-criterion sentence, blast-radius question ordering, honest quiz grading, decision checkpointing with conflict flagging, the economic stop rule, source-as-spec reference reading, and the three-line plan opening.
- Nico Bailon — https://github.com/nicobailon/grill-for-unknowns (MIT). Adapted: the unknowns-ledger concept, the question budget, the fatigue valve, and the material/grounded/answerable question gates.
- Shanghai AI Lab — StraTA (arXiv 2605.06642): plan-conditioned execution and plan/execution credit attribution; Self-Harness (arXiv 2606.09498): trace-mined, regression-gated environment evolution.

What gap-skills adds over its sources: one gap primitive spanning cognitive and harness gaps in a single ledger; the strength hierarchy (musts as physics, prose for judgment only); the dual merge gate (machine + understanding); a mechanically checkable stop condition compiled by planning; and gated, revertible environment evolution at human timescale.
